
## Name

ACDC AI

## Description

ACDC AI is your agentic talent manager. Every superstar has his own personal manager who is in charge. Simple people don't. I want to give it to everyone who is looking for a job right now. He tracks your chatgpt/claude activity, enriches your profile with your current interests and helps recruiters to better match you to the profiles companies are looking for.

## The problem

Unless you're AI researcher or have 7+ years of experience in one particular field, it's nearly impossible to get an interview if you're looking for a job right now.

AI changed the skills composition forever but the job boards are still static and archaic.
Recruiters make decisions based on whether the candidate has a "sexy company name on your CV", spactacular Linkedin or has tons of experience with a particular skillset.
Recruiters can't understand the way you do your job. Recruiters do not understand how you use LLM. Recruiters can't evaluate the vision, the approach, the result. And any professional that know how to leverage AI is 100x more efficient than employee that don't.
So how can we help recruiters to make better decisions? We need to show them our chat log history of projects we're the most proud of. and we have our AI agents that can explain, adn talk to recruiters agent and they find if the approach is a fit.


## The ICP

- Mid-senior level professionals with a diverse and mixed skillset. 
- Junior-mid level professionals with an experience in one field who are transitioning to the new completely different role. 

I'm personally looking for a job right now and I'm my own ICP.
I've been investor for many years, I've been investor relation/fundraising manager at an acquired startup, I've been a founder for my own 2 projects over last 3 years, and I did end-to end custdev, GTM, IR, development, etc.

I don't fit into any classical job board posting unless I want to continue working in the VC fund and I'm way more qualified to become the intern into any specific field (like GTM, BD, or PM)

ADCD is for people like me.
